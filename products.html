Silicone Sealants

One-component silicone range in four families — alkoxy eco, weatherproof, structural and flame-retardant — for curtain walls, windows, doors, stone and decorative sealing.

Alkoxy Eco

XH-6300A · XH-6500A

Alkoxy cure, low odor & low VOC, neutral — no corrosion to coated glass. Single-component, easy gun application. Ideal for interior decoration and eco-sensitive projects.

Weatherproof

0896 · S9660 · S9680 · S9870 · SBD-9880 · XH-6050A

Neutral-cure weatherproofing for curtain walls, windows, doors and stone. Movement capability up to ±35%, UV and weather resistant (−40 °C to +150 °C).

Structural

S9790 · SBD-9090

One-component structural silicones for structural glazing and IGU secondary structural sealing. Meet GB 16776; bond to glass, metal and concrete.

Flame Retardant

S9850 · XH-6026 · XH-6027

Fire-rated joint sealing for public buildings, escape routes, fire doors and ducts. Flame class FV-0 (GB/T24267); XH-6027 additionally meets A3 fire resistance (GB23864).
