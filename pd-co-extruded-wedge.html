Co-extruded Warm Edge Spacer

Multi-layer co-extruded one-piece spacer: low thermal conductivity, stable moisture barrier.

6A–27A
6A–27A
Color: Black ,other color available as required

Multi-layer co-extruded one-piece molding, low thermal conductivity and thermal bridge interruption. Stable moisture barrier layer, UV-resistant and excellent weather resistance. Cold bendable, compatible with butyl sealant and silicone sealant. Stable dimension, reduces condensation at the edge of insulating glass and improves energy efficiency.

Request price on WhatsApp Send an inquiry

Application

Mainly used for insulating glass and triple-glazed double-cavity glass. Suitable for energy-saving glass processing of doors, windows, curtain walls and sunrooms, applicable to passive houses and energy renovation projects.

Limitation

Store away from moisture, direct sunlight and heavy pressure. Avoid damage to the surface barrier film during cutting and bending. Process under suitable temperature and humidity. Ensure compatibility with matched sealants. Use up quickly after opening and reseal the package timely.

Product specifications

6A-27A; can also be customized according to customer requirements.

Shelf life

12 months when unopened and stored in cool & dry place. Use promptly after opening. Do not use for production if damp and invalid. Instruction:

  • Cut to size and bend corners as required;
  • Clean the contact surfaces of glass and warm edge spacer, keep dry and dust-free;
  • Apply butyl sealant and position to attach to glass;
  • Combine and press the panes, apply secondary sealant, cure and condition.

Maintenance

No separate maintenance required for finished insulating glass. Inspect the glass interior regularly. Fogging or condensation indicates seal failure, the whole unit shall be replaced. Clean with neutral detergent; strong acid and alkali are prohibited.

Safety notes

This product is non-toxic and will not harm the human body. When handling the warm edge spacer, wear clean and non-shedding gloves to avoid leaving fingerprints or stains. The warm edge spacer requires a one-piece bending process using professional bending tools. For non-bending connections, ensure the corner keys used match the size specifications to prevent issues such as air leakage or molecular sieve leakage.
