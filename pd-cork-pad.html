Cork Pad (EVA)

EVA pads that isolate glass panes during storage and transport.

EVA pads
EVA pads

Cork pads serve as isolators during the storage and transportation of insulating glass. They are usually used to prevent scratching, scraping, and breakage caused by contact or collision between glass surfaces. They can effectively protect the glass, keeping it intact and undamaged.

Product features

Effectively reduce pressure on the insulating glass during transportation.

  • The cork pad is easy to adhere to the glass surface. It has strong tensile resistance, high toughness, excellent shock absorption, and good slip resistance and cushioning.
  • It is aesthetic, safe, and easy to install,. It can adhere to glass for extended periods and leaving no residue when removed.

Application

Provide isolation and protection for the insulating glass during the storage and transportation.
