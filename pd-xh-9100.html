Two-Component Silicone Structural Sealant for IGU

XH-9100 · Insulating Glass Accessories

Two-component, dealcoholized, neutral-curing silicones for IGU secondary sealing: high modulus and high strength once mixed in the specified ratio on a dedicated two-component machine. Component A in 190 L iron drums, component B in 19 L plastic drums. Application: secondary sealing of IGU · secondary sealing of structural IGU · other glass sealing and bonding.

Specifications

ItemValue
Tack-free time1.2 h
Shore A hardness30–60
Tensile at 23 °C (MPa)≥ 0.6 MPa
Tensile at 90 °C (MPa)≥ 0.45 MPa
Tensile at −30 °C (MPa)≥ 0.45 MPa
After water immersion (MPa)≥ 0.45 MPa
After water-UV (MPa)≥ 0.45 MPa
Mass loss1.7 %

Packing: A component 190 L iron drum · B component 19 L plastic drum. Storage: below 27 °C, cool and dry — A component 12 months, B component 9 months. Safety: refer to the model’s SDS.

Key features

  • Excellent adhesion to glass and coated glass
  • Two-component, controllable cure, fast deep curing
  • Low water-vapour and gas permeability
  • Excellent temperature and weathering resistance
  • Neutral fast cure, non-corrosive
  • No batch-matching restriction
