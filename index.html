Company profile

Jiangxi Chenshuo Adhesive Industry Co., Ltd. was founded in 2016. We are a professional one-stop supplier of insulating glass consumables based in Yingtan, Jiangxi, China. Our main products include silicone sealant, butyl sealant, aluminum spacer bars, molecular sieves and other supporting accessories for insulated glass production.

We maintain long-term and stable partnerships with multiple qualified manufacturers. We also hold long-term shareholdings in two major sealant production bases in Guangdong and Jiangxi, giving us direct production control and stable supply. We conduct strict pre-shipment quality inspections, customize product selection according to customers’ technical requirements and budgets, and offer consolidated shipment as well as unified after-sales service.

Instead of contacting multiple factories separately, customers can source all insulating glass raw materials from us in one stop. Visits to our cooperative factories can be arranged upon request. We are committed to delivering stable, cost-effective and reliable sealing solutions for insulating glass processors and distributors worldwide.

Chenshuo is committed to one-stop supporting services for insulating glass customers, and sincerely welcomes customers from home and abroad to visit and cooperate with us for long-term win-win growth.
