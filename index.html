About us

13+ years professional insulating glass consumables manufacturer

CHENSHUO adheres to the quality concept that "quality is the life of an enterprise". The company provides one-stop insulating glass consumables services to help customers save both time and money — one container holds all required items. Its products comply with GB/T, JC/T and other building materials industry standards, and have obtained a variety of quality management certificates including ISO 9001.

  • Large scale: Two share-held production bases in Hubei and Guangdong for direct production control and stable supply.
  • Product: Complete product range, providing users with comprehensive sealant services.
  • Experienced: Focused on sales and production of organic silicone for more than 13 years.
  • Quality Assurance: Professional laboratory personnel conduct quality testing before shipment to strictly control product quality.
  • Efficient Service: Professional service team, 24-hour quick response service.
