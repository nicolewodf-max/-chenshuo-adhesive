About Us

Chenshuo is a manufacturer of insulating glass consumables — producing silicone sealant, butyl sealant, aluminum spacer bars and molecular sieve under one roof.

Company profile

Jiangxi Chenshuo Adhesive Co., Ltd. is a manufacturing company focused on consumables for insulating glass (IG) production. Our range covers the four essentials an IG line uses every day: silicone sealant, hot-melt butyl sealant, aluminum spacer bars and molecular sieve.

Because we manufacture rather than trade, customers deal directly with the producing plant — which means consistent batches, clearer communication and simpler purchasing for glass processors and distributors.

Quality control

Quality is managed at every stage — from incoming material to the packed carton.

  1. 01

    Incoming material inspection

    Raw materials are checked against specification when they arrive at the plant.

  2. 02

    Controlled production

    Formulations and process parameters follow set recipes, with batch records kept for traceability.

  3. 03

    In-process sampling

    Samples are taken during production and checked against the target values.

  4. 04

    Final batch inspection

    Each batch is reviewed and recorded before packing and shipment.
